COMMON CHALLENGES

Built to solve what asset owners face

Safe and cost-effective alternatives to human saturation diving at depths exceeding 100 meters are rare. Our autonomous units provide risk-free data collection in extreme environments without the need for life support systems.

Corrosion, fatigue cracks, and coating breakdown progress unseen below the waterline. Continuous, structured inspection surfaces degradation early — before it becomes failure.

Mobilizing dive teams and support vessels is slow and expensive. A single modular platform captures the same data in a fraction of the time and cost.

Raw footage leaves gaps. Every dive returns geo-referenced, structured findings, so nothing critical goes unrecorded or unreviewed.
