Wall Thickness Measurement

Know Your Remaining Metal, Everywhere That Matters.

  • UT
  • PAUT
  • PEC
Request a Scope

Description

We measure the wall thickness of pipelines, vessel shells, jacket legs, and any hollow steel member — without cutting, draining, or removing coatings. Precise thickness profiles identify corrosion and calculate remaining service life.

What You Receive

Digital thickness maps, geo-referenced point-measurement data tables, and color-coded anomaly flagging against nominal wall thresholds.

Corrosion Mapping

Visualize the Invisible Decay of Assets.

  • PAUT
  • EC
  • PEC
Request a Scope

Description

Utilizing Phased Array and Eddy Current sensors, we generate high-resolution surface maps to detect internal and external pitting. This provides a 2D or 3D visual "heat map" of corrosion density across the entire asset surface.

What You Receive

Interactive C-Scan imaging, depth-of-pit quantification, and comparative historical datasets for rate-of-decay analysis.

Weld Inspection

Structural Integrity at Every Junction.

  • CVI
  • PAUT
  • EC
Request a Scope

Description

Specialized robotic payloads focus on critical welds. We scan for fatigue cracks, lack of fusion, and porosity in structural welds on rigs, hulls, and mooring systems, ensuring the mechanical bond remains sound under stress.

What You Receive

Detailed crack profiling, flaw sizing reports, and visual proof of weld condition via 4K UHD close-up imaging.

CP Survey

Monitor Your Asset's Shield Against the Sea.

  • CP Probe
Request a Scope

Description

Cathodic Protection (CP) surveys measure the electrical potential of the asset. We identify anode depletion levels and verify that the galvanic protection system is adequately preventing the electro-chemical corrosion of your steel.

What You Receive

Potential level charting, anode wastage percentage estimates, and pass/fail protection assessments per DNV or API standards.

Description

General Visual (GVI) and Close Visual Inspections (CVI) are the frontline of subsea monitoring. Using high-wattage lighting and 4K cameras, we document marine growth, debris, and obvious mechanical damage or deformation.
